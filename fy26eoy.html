Report basis Cube: Equipment Division Scenario: Forecast6p6 Entity: GSE (OFI Global) FY2026, last closed month 2026M6 Reported in EUR, restated at EUR/USD 1.1379 Macro shocks applied to Oct–Dec 2026 only; Jan–Sep held at the 6p6 Base figures pulled 1 October 2026
Base ties to the cube. The page derives every subtotal from the posting lines and checks net income against cube account 99999 each time it opens (see Report basis).

Volume elasticities (share of a line that flexes with volume): revenue and material COGS 100%; freight 100%; labour COGS 70%; utilities 60%; warehouse 50%; overhead COGS 40%; R&M credit 30%; supplemental 20%; compensation 10% (overtime and commission only); professional services, HR, facility, telecom, D&A fixed.

Crude pass-through: freight 0.60, utilities 0.50, warehouse 0.10, overhead COGS 0.15 of the crude move.

FX — one driver, not two. The cube reports USD, but USD is only the reporting currency: the four operating entities are functionally EUR (NL13 Netherlands, ES10 Spain), IDR (ID10) and MYR (MY10). Restating the book into EUR at a plan rate is arithmetic that scales every line and every scenario identically — it moves no conclusion, so it sits in the box above the table rather than in the assumptions. The only genuine FX risk is the non-EUR functional currencies moving against EUR, which is the single FX vs EUR slider. It applies to the non-EUR share of each block — revenue 29.9%, COGS 17.7%, opex 39.0%, from the FY26 entity split.

Because Malaysia carries the manufacturing cost base while revenue books in the Dutch and Spanish entities, a weaker IDR and MYR is a small net benefit: it cuts more cost than revenue. That asymmetry, 39% of cost against 30% of revenue, is a structural hedge already on the books.

Interest rate. Account 85999 has appeared and been cleared more than once in this cube. Where it carries a balance, the rate driver scales it by the repricing share of borrowings (60% by default) times the rate change relative to the current average rate (5.0% by default) — both estimates, not cube data, since the planning scenarios hold no debt schedule. Where the account is empty, as at the current refresh, the driver has nothing to scale and reports zero. Incremental working capital from higher material prices is excluded throughout, as sizing it needs a balance sheet this forecast does not contain.

Tax. Incremental profit taxed at the FY26 effective rate on profit before tax of 36.9%. The Q4 booked rate in the 6p6 is 49.7% and is phasing-distorted, so it is not used as the marginal rate.

Switching assumptions off. A driver that is switched off is held at its 6p6 value, so its contribution becomes exactly zero and the remaining drivers are re-decomposed against the same base. The scenario comparison chart applies the same on/off mask to all five scenarios, so it stays like-for-like.

Spot reference: EUR/USD 1.1379 at 24 Sep 2026. Brent Q4-26 sell-side range 70–80.
